## Design Documentation

Before implementing significant architectural changes or new features, create or update a design document under `docs/designs/`.

Design documents should explain:

- The purpose and intended behavior.
- The proposed architecture and interfaces.
- Important design decisions and trade-offs.
- Relevant invariants and constraints.

Keep documentation concise and proportional to the complexity of the change.

Consult existing design documents before modifying related components. Update them whenever implementation changes invalidate their contents.

For significant architectural changes, present the design for review before implementation.
